## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-craft corporate workspace and social-ordering finance hub. It blends the structural rigor of enterprise fintech dashboards with the agility and accessibility of an on-demand office beverage app. The experience serves fast-paced modern companies where group drink runs, expense balancing, automated tab splits, and room-based batch orders need to be executed with zero friction and absolute fiscal transparency.

The visual style is rooted in **Corporate / Modern High-Precision Minimalist**. It avoids playful consumer clutter in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, refined micro-surfaces, balanced data density, and reassuring financial legibility. The interface evokes feelings of corporate poise, clarity, fiscal reliability, and seamless teamwork. 

Subtle tactile feedback, architectural bento containers, crisp boundary strokes, and purposeful status-driven accents elevate utility while keeping financial states—paid, pending, settling, and locked—instantly readable at a glance.

## Colors

The color system delivers stark contrast, razor-sharp edge definition, and unmistakable semantic signaling tailored for ledger visibility and order coordination.

- **Primary Canvas & Slate Tones:** Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) commands high-emphasis typography, primary action surfaces, and active navigation nodes. Mid-Slate (`#1E293B`) and Muted Slate (`#334155`) govern structural card headers, secondary headers, and structural icons.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base surface for modular cards and interactive panels, set against a pristine, distraction-free app background of Crisp Canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Border definitions across all containers use precise Hairline Slate (`#E2E8F0`), maintaining structural rhythm without visual weight. Subdued helper copy and meta captions utilize Slate Neutral (`#64748B`).
- **Semantic Accents (Finance & Settlement):** Fresh Mint / Emerald (`#10B981`, paired with deep `#059669` for text contrast on light fills) signals settled balances, successful VietQR transactions, zero-debt confirmations, and active delivery arrivals.
- **Semantic Accents (Attention & Active Flow):** Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`, paired with deep `#D97706`) designates unsettled shared debts, live pooling timers, pending room approvals, and checkout lock countdowns.
- **Functional Fill Tiers:** Status badges and callouts always deploy an 8%–12% tint background paired with full-saturation foreground glyphs and high-contrast text (e.g., `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` foreground for Mint; `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` foreground for Amber).

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve geometric clarity paired with corporate humanism. Its contemporary vertical proportions and distinct open counters maintain ultra-crisp fidelity on handheld viewports and dense desktop matrices alike.

### Financial Tabular Metrics
Crucial to this hub is tabular data alignment. All numeric components, including balance amounts, payment tabs, item pricing, countdown clocks, and custom topping tallies, strictly employ proportional lining numbers with tabular figures enabled via OpenType feature tag `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`. This eliminates visual jitter during dynamic real-time pool updates.

### Hierarchy Rules
- `headline-xl` / `headline-xl-mobile`: Reserved for top-level workspace balances, primary room entry headers, and monthly consolidated totals.
- `headline-sm` & `label-lg`: Power section divides, modal titles, and bento card headlines.
- `numeric-stat`: Dedicated to central metrics inside financial cards (e.g., "₫ 145.000" debt or room contribution totals).
- `label-sm`: Standard uppercase micro-copy (letter-spacing +0.04em, line-height 14px) for status indicators, order batch states, and topping configuration labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a predictable 8-point spatial cadence (augmented with 4px half-steps for micro-alignments inside pills and tags).

### Layout System
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 640px):** Single-column fluid stacked layout. 16px lateral outer margins. Fixed bottom tab navigation docking with unified 80px bottom safe-area offset.
- **Tablet (641px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid responsive grid. 24px margins and gutters. Side-by-side split view: Active Room Orders on 4 columns, Personal Ledger & VietQR action docked in 2 columns.
- **Desktop & Workspace Hub (>1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (capped at 1280px container width). Asymmetric bento architecture: central catalog stream (6 cols), sticky live room pool state (3 cols), and personal financial balance module (3 cols).

### Bento Grid Principles
Bento compartments utilize strict internal padding:
- **Dense Bento (Status / Actions):** 16px internal padding (`space-base`).
- **Standard Bento (Order Summary / Drink Configuration):** 20px to 24px internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- Gap consistency is uniform across all grid cards at 16px (`space-base`) to maintain clean gutters and cohesive layout lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **Micro-Border & Tinted Elevation** framework. Rather than heavy, muddy drop shadows, depth is achieved through layered background shades, razor-thin structural borders, and subtle downward directional lighting.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, non-interactive foundation.
- **Level 1 (Bento Cards & Inset Panels):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` with a precise `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Room Switcher Popovers, Hover Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, VietQR Checkout Drawer, Mobile Bottom Dock):** `#FFFFFF` anchored with an elevated edge. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

### Glass & Active Lock Treatments
When the room switches to "Locked / In Kitchen", floating indicators use a high-order glass layer: `background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, with an internal inset border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape identity, curating high-trust geometry with soft, approachable interactive terminals.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Segment Selectors, Bento Modules):** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers structured neatness across form controls and drink option matrices.
- **Containers & Major Bento Modules:** Medium-large radius of `1rem` (16px) creates architectural structure without feeling boxy.
- **Pills & Floating Identifiers:** Full radius (`9999px`) applied to room switchers, counter tags, VietQR launch capsules, and financial status pills. This contrasts sharply against rectangular bento modules to draw quick navigation focus.

## Components

### 1. Room Switcher Dropdown Pill
- **Structure:** Floating capsule pill (`height: 38px`, padding: `0 14px`). Background `#FFFFFF`, stroke `1px solid #E2E8F0`, subtle shadow Level 1.
- **Content:** Team icon on left (`16px`), room name in `label-md` `#0F172A` (e.g., "Engineering Floor 4"), small emerald ping indicator dot (`6px`) showing live activity, followed by chevron-down icon.
- **Expanded State:** Opens a compact Level 2 popover listing subscribed workspace rooms with unread drink round indicators and active timers.

### 2. Debt Summary & VietQR Quick Action Widget
- **Visual Card:** Compact bento module with a crisp `#F8FAFC` inner chamber bounded by `#E2E8F0`.
- **Primary Metric:** Displays user status prominently: "You Owe" in Amber (`#D97706`) or "All Settled" in Mint (`#059669`) with tabular `numeric-stat`.
- **VietQR Action Trigger:** Full-width or inline rounded button (`#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-md`). Includes an embedded mini-QR glyph and chevron. Tapping triggers the instant scan-to-pay bottom sheet pre-filled with the exact dong amount and bank memo syntax (`ROOM_[ID]_USER_[ID]`).

### 3. Single Active Order Lock Indicator
- **Status Mechanism:** Renders at the top of the room feed when a round is finalized.
- **Visuals:** Dark architectural bar (`#0F172A`) with a subtle glowing Amber boundary accent (`#F59E0B`). Contains a pad-lock vector, bold label "Order Locked for Dispatch", and a real-time countdown timer in tabular font (`04:18`).
- **Interactive State:** Disables customization edits across drink lines; displays the appointed courier or admin who holds checkout ownership.

### 4. Beverage Customization Form Elements
- **Sugar & Ice Selectors:** Multi-segment pill sliders. Inactive state: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text. Active selected state: `#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, with micro-scale spring transitions.
- **Topping Checkboxes & Add-ons:** Custom rounded-rect chips with dual labels: item name (`body-sm`) left-aligned, calculated differential (`+5.000₫` in `numeric-table`) right-aligned. Selected state applies `#ECFDF5` background with `#059669` border and checkmark.

### 5. Buttons & Interactive States
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Slate (`#0F172A`), hover state `#1E293B`, active `#020617`, text `#FFFFFF`. Corner radius 8px (`rounded-md`).
- **Secondary / Success Action:** Emerald Action (`#10B981`), hover state `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`. Corner radius 8px.
- **Outline Action:** `#FFFFFF` background, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`, hover background `#F8FAFC`.

### 6. Mobile Bottom Dock Navigation
- **Dock Bar:** Fixed bottom bar (`height: 64px`), background `#FFFFFF/95` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Top border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Navigation Items:** 4 distinct tabs: "Rooms", "Order Flow", "My Debts", "Activity". Active tab features a 3px top indicator notch in `#0F172A` paired with medium-weight iconography. Unsettled balance badges render on the "My Debts" tab in Amber (`#F59E0B`).
